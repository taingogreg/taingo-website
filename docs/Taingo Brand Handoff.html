01 · Logo files

02 · Color palette · Copper & Midnight

Three roles: Midnight for surfaces and primary type, Copper for the accent and CTAs, Sun for highlights and warmth. Paper and Bone are the light surfaces.

When to use which

Copper drives action.
Buttons, links, the things people tap. If it's not interactive, it shouldn't be copper.
Sun adds warmth.
Status pills, ratings, KPI numbers, the "You grow" half of the tagline on dark backgrounds. Never use Sun for primary actions — it competes with copper.
Midnight grounds.
Type, headers, surfaces that need authority. Pair with Paper for the lightweight site mode and with Sun for highlight moments.
Use Copper Light on dark backgrounds.
Regular Copper goes muddy on Midnight. Swap to Copper Light (#E07142) when the surface is dark.

03 · Typography

Fraunces display

Headlines, the wordmark, hero copy. Weight 600 for the wordmark; weight 800 or 900 for big hero text. Use italic at weight 400 for the "ai" treatment and the "You grow" tagline accent.

We answer.
You grow.

Manrope body

Everything else — body text, nav, buttons, forms. Weights 500 (body), 600 (buttons / emphasis), 700–800 (subheads).

Subhead in Manrope 700
Body copy in Manrope 500. Comfortable for paragraphs, easy at small sizes, plays nicely beneath Fraunces headings.

07 · Voice & usage

Tagline: "We answer. You grow."
Always two sentences with a period after each. The accent color goes on "You grow" only. Don't shorten it to "we answer, you grow" — the periods do the work.
Logo lockup with tagline.
Stack the wordmark above a 3px copper rule, with the tagline below in spaced caps (Manrope 500, letter-spacing 0.32em). Or set the tagline beside the wordmark in Fraunces 600 with the accent on "You grow".
The italic "ai" is the brand.
Wherever Taingo appears in body copy on the site, mark it up with the same kerning and copper italic — it's small but consistent, and it reinforces the wordmark.
Talk like the trades.
Short sentences, plain words. "Never miss a call." "Books jobs while you're on a roof." Avoid SaaS-ese.
